What This Shows

Price did not buy quality on this test. Leave every box checked and set the bar at 85%: the cheapest model that clears it costs about 1% as much as the most expensive one. Then check only "SSP narrative review," the hardest category, and watch the leader change. A single leaderboard number hides that. The right model depends on the work.

That's how I'd approach any AI rollout. Write the real tasks, grade the answers against a rubric, and choose on score and cost together, one job at a time. Want the method for your own work? Email chris@cmm.dev. More work samples.
